Título: Sixpence None The Richer’s original line-up share first new song in 20 years, ‘We Are Love’

Sixpence None The Richer have shared ‘We Are Love’, marking the first new song with the group’s original line-up in 20 years.
The song is the first offering from a new EP from the US alt-rock band, who are best known for their singles ‘Kiss Me’ (1998) and ‘Breathe Your Name’ (2002). Their new EP ‘Rosemary Hill’ is set for release on October 4 via Flatiron. You can pre-order it here.
Featuring a twangy acoustic guitar paired with thunderous harmonies and sharp electric guitar riffs, “We Are Love” sees frontwoman Leigh Nash sing: “We are love, we can make it
We’re the chain you can’t break it / The long darkness had arrived / But hate here comes to sight / We are love, we can take it.”

Sixpence None The Richer’s sixth and most recent studio album, ‘Lost In Transition’, was released in 2012. Their new track comes a month after the group announced that they will be embarking on a 2024 ‘We Are Love’ 25th anniversary North American tour.
